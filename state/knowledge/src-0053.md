Collected by cycle 148 (T1) as a **third, academically-bylined carrier** of the South Africa case
(`src-0049`, `src-0050`) — and, more importantly, as **a documented instance of an institution's
HEDGED causal statement being restated without its hedge by a downstream commentator**. Published
**29 April 2026** by **The Conversation**, bylined **Nomalanga Mashinini, Senior Lecturer, University
of the Witwatersrand**. Fetched at the **direct render only** (one render family).

**THE PARAPHRASE-DRIFT DATUM, WHICH IS THE MAIN REASON THIS SOURCE WAS KEPT.** `src-0049` establishes

citations were included without proper verification."** This page renders the minister's position as:

# Key claims

1. **A DOCUMENTED HEDGE-STRIPPING EVENT BETWEEN A FIRST-PARTY INSTITUTIONAL STATEMENT AND ITS
   DOWNSTREAM RESTATEMENT, THREE DAYS APART.** See the Summary for the two wordings side by side.
   This matters to this base beyond the South Africa case: it is **direct evidence for the standing
   discipline that a claim must be taken from the closest available artefact**, and it is the
   mechanism by which a *hedged* institutional attribution would silently become an *unhedged* one in
   any downstream tally. **The drift runs in the direction of a stronger causal claim, which is the
   direction this base is least able to detect by internal consistency checks alone.**
2. **AN INDEPENDENT NAMING OF THE DISCOVERY ROUTE AS A NEWSROOM.** This page names **News24** as the
   party that checked the references — **"Journalists checked the references and found that they
   contained fabrications."** This is a **third** page naming News24, alongside `src-0050` and a
   `dailymaverick.co.za` render, and it is the only one of the three written by an academic rather
   than a newsroom. **No AI-detection tool or vendor is named anywhere on this page** (ABSENT, asked
   directly without an answer supplied).
3. **A TAXONOMY OF THE FABRICATIONS, AND A PUBLICATION DATE FOUND NOWHERE ELSE IN THIS BASE.** The
   page states the draft policy was **published on 10 April 2026** — a date **ABSENT from all three
   other South Africa pages cycle 148 fetched**, and therefore **single-source and single-render;
   flagged as such and not to be relied on without a second render**. It also states the fabrications
   fell into **two kinds**: citations to **academic journals that do not exist**, and citations to
   **real journals in which the referenced articles were never published**. **This two-kind
   distinction is not recorded anywhere else in this base** and is a finer-grained failure taxonomy
   than the *broken link / misattributed / fabricated* categories the EY and KPMG material uses.

# Limitations

- **AN OPINION/ANALYSIS PIECE BY A SINGLE ACADEMIC, NOT A REPORT OF PRIMARY FINDINGS.** The
  Conversation publishes academic commentary. Its factual content on this incident is **entirely
  downstream** of News24's investigation and the minister's statement.
- **ONE RENDER FAMILY ONLY.** Every ABSENT recorded here is a **one-render** absent.
- **ITS RENDERING OF THE MINISTER IS DEMONSTRABLY NOT VERBATIM.** See key claim 1. **This page is
  therefore a source about the incident's reception, not a source for what the minister said.**
- **NO COUNTS.** Asked directly, the page returned **ABSENT** for the total reference count, the
  number fabricated, the page count, the journal names and any editor statement — all of which
  search-result summaries offered. **None of those figures is a claim of this base.**
- **THE 10 APRIL 2026 PUBLICATION DATE IS SINGLE-SOURCE AND SINGLE-RENDER.** `mg.co.za` independently
  gives the public-comment deadline as **10 June**, which is consistent with a 10 April gazetting and
  a 60-day comment window, but **consistency is not corroboration** and cycle 148 does not treat the
  April date as established.
- **NOT INDEPENDENT OF `src-0049`/`src-0050` AS TO THE UNDERLYING FACTS.** One incident, one
  ministerial statement, one investigation. Newsroom and author diversity across `src-0049`,
  `src-0050` and `src-0053` is **carrier diversity, not evidential independence**.
