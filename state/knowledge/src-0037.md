# Summary

The GPTZero investigation into **PwC Middle East**, published **28 July 2026** — the most recent
instalment of the sweep and, at eight days before this fetch, the freshest artefact in this base.
It documents a **fifth named institution** for half (A) of
`institutional-incident-real-world-impact`, and it differs from every prior instalment in one
structural respect that matters more than the incident count:

**IT INVESTIGATES FOUR REPORTS AT ONCE AND ASSERTS A PATTERN ACROSS THEM WITHIN A SINGLE
PUBLISHER.** Prior instalments took one report from one firm. This one names four PwC "thought
leadership" reports and states verbatim that "these reports show a pattern of irresponsible AI
usage". That is a **within-publisher** repeat-offence finding, which is a different and stronger
claim than four unrelated single incidents — and it is the closest thing this base has yet seen to
a *rate over a named publisher's output*, while still falling well short of one (see Limitations).

Two of the four reports are squarely **cyber/CTI-adjacent** — *Building a Cyber-Resilient
eMobility Ecosystem* and, by subject, the governance and mobility work — which makes this the
closest any GPTZero instalment has come to the topic's actual population.

**IT RECORDS NO INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE.** Asked directly for PwC's response and for any direct
quotation from PwC, the page returned **ABSENT for both**. Unlike KPMG, cycle 99 found **no
response artefact for PwC at all** and did not search for one — see Limitations. Half (B)
therefore gains an incident **without** a response, and stays at **N=3 recorded responses**
(ENISA, EY, KPMG), not N=4.

# Key claims

1. **A FIFTH NAMED INSTITUTION, AND THE FIRST WITHIN-PUBLISHER PATTERN CLAIM: GPTZero reports
   hallucinated citations, fabricated claims and drafting defects across FOUR named PwC Middle
   East "thought leadership" reports** — *Transforming Governance: Citizen Pulse: Delivering
   Real-Time Insights to Meet Society's Evolving Needs*; *Shaping the GCC Mobility Landscape –
   Electric, Connected and Autonomous*; *Building a Cyber-Resilient eMobility Ecosystem*; and
   *Agentic AI – The New Frontier in GenAI*. Verbatim: "Together, these reports show a pattern of
   irresponsible AI usage resulting in hallucinated (vibe) citations, fabricated claims, and
   incomprehensible drafting and formatting decisions."
2. **THE CITATION DEFECT REPORTED FOR THE GOVERNANCE REPORT IS A STRUCTURAL ONE — UNANCHORED
   REFERENCES — WHICH IS A DIFFERENT FAILURE MODE FROM THE DEAD LINKS THIS BASE HAS TRACKED.**
   Verbatim: "Only 10 of the 17 citations in the References section are numbered and actually
   correspond to footnotes in the body of the report", and "Another seven lack numbers and
   associated footnotes, meaning they aren't linked to specific claims." The defect is that
   references **do not attach to any claim**, rather than that they fail to resolve. A
   link-resolution sweep of the kind `open_questions[0]` proposes **would not detect this**, which
   compounds the same point src-0036 makes about third-party-denied fabricated claims.
3. **GPTZERO REPORTS AN AI-DETECTOR SCORE FOR THIS REPORT, AND IT IS READABLE IN TEXT MODE:
   "According to our AI Detector, there is an 84% chance that Transforming Governance is entirely
   AI-generated", rising to 100% "If the endmatter (including references) is ignored."** This is
   the **detector's own output**, with all of src-0032's standing vendor caveats attached, and it
   is **not** a finding about citation accuracy. It is recorded here chiefly because
   `open_questions[7]` flags a numeral-collision hazard around GPTZero detector scores: **this
   page's 84% is plainly labelled as a detector probability**, which is the labelling clarity the
   EY page's unreadable odometer denied cycle 90.

# Limitations

- **SINGLE FETCH, ONE RENDER FAMILY — WEAKER THAN src-0034 ON EXACTLY THE AXIS THAT MATTERS.**
  Direct render only. **No `r.jina.ai` pair-render was run**, so unlike the KPMG counts, **none of
  the numerals in this file (17, 10, seven, 84%, 100%) has been two-render confirmed.** Given that
  src-0032's odometer defect and src-0034's disagreeing percentage tiles both concerned numerals
  on this same vendor's pages, **these figures should be treated as provisional until pair-rendered.**
  The direct render did report that no numeral appeared as a digit run, which is mildly
  reassuring and not a substitute.
- **THE "PATTERN" CLAIM HAS NO DENOMINATOR EITHER.** Four reports are named as defective; **how
  many PwC reports were scanned to find them is not stated.** `open_questions[0]`'s finding stands
  entirely unaffected: this is still numerators-only, serially released, and it **cannot** be used
  to compute a rate for PwC or for anyone. Cycle 99 considered whether a within-publisher pattern
  claim reopens that question and concluded it does not, and **no G3 contradiction was opened.**
- **NO INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE, AND NONE WAS SOUGHT.** The page returned ABSENT for PwC's response
  and for any PwC quotation. **Cycle 99 did not run a separate search for a PwC response** — the
  source budget was exhausted and the KPMG response was the higher-value target. **Searching for
  a PwC response is a named, cheap, entirely untried route that could take half (B) from N=3 to
  N=4**, and is the single most valuable follow-up this source leaves behind.
- **VENDOR PAGE.** Same standing caveat as src-0032 and src-0034: GPTZero sells the detector whose
  output claim 3 reports. The 84%/100% figures are marketing-adjacent and must never be cited as
  an independent measurement of AI authorship.
- **"fabricated claims" IS NOT ITEMISED IN WHAT WAS EXTRACTED.** The headline promises hallucinated
  "product and government customers" — i.e. the src-0036 failure mode of false assertions about
  named third parties — but **cycle 99 did not extract which customers were named, and no third
  party is on record here denying anything.** Do not transfer src-0036's third-party-denial
  finding to PwC; it is not established.
- **RECENCY CUTS BOTH WAYS.** At eight days old on the access date, this story may not yet have
  drawn a response that a later cycle would find. Absence of a PwC response as of 2026-08-05 is
  **not** evidence of a decision not to respond, and must not be scored as one.
- **SCOPE CAVEAT:** PwC Middle East is a consultancy, not a CTI publisher proper, though *Building
  a Cyber-Resilient eMobility Ecosystem* is nearer the topic's population than any prior instalment.
