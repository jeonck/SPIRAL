## Summary

ENISA's own publication page for the report at the centre of the src-0004 incident.
Fetched in cycle 12 as a PRIMARY-SOURCE check on secondary reporting (Heise citing Der
Spiegel). Its value is twofold and mostly NEGATIVE, which is the point: it independently
confirms that a corrective revision happened, on the date related reporting claimed, and
that the defect was link-related — while containing no acknowledgement of AI involvement
and no figure for the number of bad footnotes.

## Key claims

1. The page carries a verbatim revision notice: "Revision Notice – Version 1.2.
   (09 January 2026): This publication has been updated to edit some links." The
   downloadable file is `ENISA Threat Landscape 2025_v1.2.pdf`, served from a `2026-01/`
   path, against a stated publication date of October 1, 2025. This is ENISA's own
   confirmation, independent of Der Spiegel and Heise, that the October 2025 report was
   revised on 2026-01-09 and that the revision concerned links.
2. The page contains NO statement that AI, artificial intelligence or generative AI was
   used in producing the report (verified as absent on 2026-07-29, ~8 months after the
   incident became public and ~6 months after the v1.2 correction). ENISA's own public
   correction record therefore describes the fix in purely mechanical terms ("edit some
   links") and does not disclose the AI involvement that ENISA separately admitted to
   Der Spiegel when asked.
3. The page provides NO count of incorrect footnotes. The 26-of-492 figure is not
   corroborated here.

## Relevant quotes

- "Revision Notice – Version 1.2. (09 January 2026): This publication has been updated
  to edit some links."
- Fetch result for AI disclosure, recorded verbatim: "ABSENT: No statement about use of
  AI, artificial intelligence, or generative AI in producing the report."
- Fetch result for methodology/footnote disclosure: "ABSENT: No explicit disclosure or
  methodology statement about sources and footnotes provided on this page."

## Limitations of this source

- An ABSENCE established by one automated fetch of a rendered page. It is good evidence
  that no AI disclosure is prominent on the landing page; it is NOT proof that no such
  statement exists inside the v1.2 PDF itself (the PDF's legal-notice/acknowledgements
  pages were not pulled this cycle). A future cycle wanting to make the non-disclosure
  claim strongly should pull the PDF front matter directly.
- The revision notice does not say WHY the links needed editing, so it corroborates the
  existence and nature of the defect but attributes no cause. It neither supports nor
  contradicts the AI attribution in src-0004; it is silent.
- Confirms nothing about the 26/492 figure.
