# Summary

The Institut für Internet-Sicherheit — if(is) at Westfälische Hochschule Gelsenkirchen is the
institution whose researchers discovered the ENISA dead-link/AI-usage incident. This is that
institute's **own website's** post about the discovery. Open question [2] of
`institutional-incident-real-world-impact` named "locating Prof. Christian Dietrich's /
Institut fuer Internet-Sicherheit's own writeup" as **the most promising untried route** to the
26-of-492 footnote figure, and specifically as "the only one that could reach the number
INDEPENDENTLY of Der Spiegel rather than by recounting ENISA's own artefacts."

**THE ROUTE IS NOW TRIED, AND THE HEADLINE RESULT IS NEGATIVE FOR ITS STATED PURPOSE.** The page
exists, it is on the institute's own domain, it names Dietrich and quotes him directly — but it
is a **re-post credited to another outlet**. It carries the line `Quelle: Der Standard
(8. Januar 2026)`. derStandard is one of the three outlets the base already holds as
*non-independent* corroboration, because derStandard itself cites Der Spiegel. **The institute's
own site therefore does not supply an independent path to the number; it relays the same
single-outlet chain.** It also does not state the number at all.

What the page *does* add, and it is not nothing: a **verbatim Dietrich quotation** in German
that the base has not held before, an **institute-domain restatement of the ~5% rate**, and a
**verbatim restatement of ENISA's own causal attribution** (AI used for "kleineren
redaktionellen Überarbeitungen"; working links altered by "menschliche Fehler").

Fetched at cycle 99 on **two render families** (direct and `r.jina.ai`). Both returned the same
body sentences verbatim. **They disagree on the date** — see Limitations.

# Key claims

1. **THE if(is) WRITEUP IS NOT AN INDEPENDENT SOURCE FOR THE ENISA FOOTNOTE NUMBER, AND THE
   CYCLE-72 ROUTE NAMED IN open_question [2] IS NOW SPENT.** The page carries the attribution
   line `Quelle: Der Standard (8. Januar 2026)` and contains **no outbound links to news
   outlets** (asked directly; ABSENT). Both render families returned **`'Spiegel'` ABSENT**,
   **`'492'` ABSENT** and **`'26'` ABSENT** from the whole page. The institute's own domain thus
   recounts derStandard, which recounts Der Spiegel; it does not reach the 26/492 figure at all,
   independently or otherwise. The figure continues to rest exactly where src-0004 and cycle 72
   put it.
2. **THE ~5% RATE IS RESTATED ON THE DISCOVERING INSTITUTE'S OWN DOMAIN, VERBATIM: "In einem der
   Berichte führten rund fünf Prozent der angegebenen Quellen auf nicht erreichbare
   Internetseiten."** ("In one of the reports, around five percent of the cited sources led to
   unreachable web pages.") This is arithmetically consistent with the base's 26/492 = 5.28% but
   is **a rounded rate, not a count**, and — per claim 1 — is relayed from derStandard rather
   than independently asserted. It corroborates the *order of magnitude* and nothing finer.
3. **DIETRICH'S CRITICISM IS RECORDED VERBATIM AND IS DIRECTED AT PUBLIC-AUTHORITY
   ACCOUNTABILITY, NOT AT AI:** "Mich stört maßgeblich, dass eine öffentliche Behörde, die in
   meinen Augen die sehr wichtige Aufgabe hat, verlässliche nachvollziehbare Berichte
   herauszugeben, das in diesem Fall nicht getan hat", followed by "Man hätte nur einmal
   draufklicken müssen" ("One would only have had to click on it once"). The page also restates
   **ENISA's own attribution**: KI used for "kleineren redaktionellen Überarbeitungen", with
   working links unintentionally altered through "menschliche Fehler", and ENISA stressing that
   the reports' substantive statements were unaffected. This corroborates — on the discovering
   institute's domain, though still not independently — the cycle-72 reading that **ENISA
   volunteered a cause**.

# Limitations

- **NOT INDEPENDENT OF DER SPIEGEL.** This is the single most important limitation and the whole
  reason the source was collected. Credited `Quelle: Der Standard`; derStandard cites Der
  Spiegel. Treating this page as institute-original testimony would be an error, and any future
  cycle tempted to do so should read key claim 1 first.
- **CONTAINS NO FOOTNOTE COUNT.** `'492'` and `'26'` are ABSENT on both renders. It cannot
  corroborate or impeach 26/492; it offers only "rund fünf Prozent".
- **RENDER DISAGREEMENT ON THE DATE, UNRESOLVED.** The direct render reported the date as
  **8 January 2026**, taking it from the `Quelle:` line. The `r.jina.ai` render reported a
  publication timestamp of **2026-01-16T10:53:59+00:00**. These are not the same date and cycle
  99 does **not** adjudicate between them: the most likely reading is that 8 Jan 2026 is
  *derStandard's* publication date (carried in the credit line) and 16 Jan 2026 is *if(is)'s* own
  posting date, but that reading is inferred, not read off the page. **No cycle may cite a
  publication date for this source without repeating this disagreement.** Per the base's standing
  practice, this is recorded as a disagreement rather than silently resolved.
- **NO BYLINE** (asked directly; ABSENT). Institutional post, no named author.
- **GERMAN ONLY.** All quotations above are the source's own German. English glosses in this file
  are cycle-99 translations and are **not** verbatim source text.
- **A RELAY, NOT A REPORT.** The page does not describe the institute's methodology, sample, or
  how the dead links were found. It adds no methodological detail beyond what the base already
  holds from src-0004.
