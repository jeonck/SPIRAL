# Summary

Collected by cycle 148 (T1) as the **principal-level response** to the MAHA report incident whose
spokesperson-level response is `src-0051`. Published **24 June 2025, 12:50 p.m.**, bylined
**Margaret Manto** (NOTUS reporter and Allbritton Journalism Institute fellow). Fetched at **two
render families** — direct and `r.jina.ai` — which **agree on every element below**; the `r.jina.ai`
render self-reported its last body line, bounding its ABSENTs per [387].

**WHY IT IS A SEPARATE SOURCE FROM `src-0051`.** `src-0051` records what a **spokesperson** said in
a press statement about a document. This page records what the **cabinet secretary responsible said
under questioning by legislators**, roughly four weeks later. This base's response population has
never before separated those two levels, and the two responses here **do not say the same thing**:
the spokesperson called the errors *minor* and named no cause; the secretary called them **"wrong"**
and **"a mistake"**, said he **did not personally fact-check the report**, and **still named no
cause**.

**IT ALSO CARRIES THE ONLY AI ATTRIBUTION THIS CASE HAS, AND IT IS THIRD-HAND.** Both renders report
that **The Washington Post** found **"clear hallmarks of the use of ChatGPT in the report"**. That is
**an outside newspaper's inference, reported by a second outlet, about a document — not an
institutional admission and not a detection-vendor analysis.** `src-0051`'s two-render ABSENT for any
institutional AI attribution stands unchanged by it.

# Key claims

1. **THE PRINCIPAL CONCEDES ERROR AND DENIES CONSEQUENCE, AND NAMES NO CAUSE.** Verbatim, both
   renders, spoken by **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.** at a **House Energy and Commerce Committee
   subcommittee hearing**: **"all of the foundational assertions in that report are accurate"**;
   **"some of the citations were messed up for one day"**; **"there were no erroneous actual
   assertions"**. The reporting further states — as **reporter paraphrase with embedded quoted
   fragments, and it must not be presented as a sentence he spoke** — that **"Kennedy said he didn't
   personally fact-check the MAHA Commission Assessment and called the report's fabricated citations
   'wrong' and a 'mistake.'"** Asked directly with no answer supplied, **both renders returned ABSENT
   for any administration official confirming or denying AI use.** So the response is
   **concede-the-defect, minimise-the-impact, name-no-cause** — and the *name-no-cause* limb matches
   EY, KPMG and PwC exactly, from an institution that is **not Big Four**.
2. **THE AI ATTRIBUTION FOR THIS CASE IS EXTERNAL, INFERENTIAL AND RELAYED, AND ITS PROVENANCE MUST
   TRAVEL WITH IT.** Both renders: **The Washington Post** reporting found **"clear hallmarks of the
   use of ChatGPT in the report"**. **This base does not hold that Washington Post reporting**, has
   not fetched it, and holds **no** account of what "hallmarks" were examined or by what method.
   **ChatGPT is the only AI product named anywhere in this base's MAHA material, and it is named by a
   newspaper and not by the institution.** A successor must never restate this as *the MAHA report
   was written with ChatGPT* or as *HHS used AI*.
3. **NO APOLOGY, AND A DIRECT QUESTION LEFT UNANSWERED.** The words *apologise/apologize/apology* are
   **ABSENT from both renders**. The `r.jina.ai` render's final delivered line is: **"How does that
   happen under your leadership?" Ruiz asked. Kennedy did not answer the question.** On the apology
   dimension this case sits with EY, ENISA, KPMG, PwC and `src-0049`, and away from `src-0048`.
4. **THE REMEDY HELD AND THE REPLACEMENTS WERE THEMSELVES DISPUTED.** Both renders: the report was
   **updated, not withdrawn**, and the **replacement citations were in turn said by study authors to
   have been misinterpreted**. This is the **only case in this base where the correction itself is
   reported as defective**, and it is a distinct datum from the original fabrication.

  independent newsrooms** and must never be counted as two. What they are is **two response levels of
  one institution at two dates**, which is their value.
- **THE WASHINGTON POST FINDING IS THIRD-HAND AND UNVERIFIED HERE.** See key claim 2. This base holds
  no Washington Post artefact, no method, and no count behind "clear hallmarks". **A cycle wanting the
  AI attribution firmed up must fetch that reporting and check its provenance first** — the route is
  **recorded as open and untried**, not as commissioned.

  spokesperson statement in `src-0051` is a voluntary press response; **this** response was given in
