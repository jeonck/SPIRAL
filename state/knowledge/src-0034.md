# Summary

The GPTZero investigation into **KPMG International's** October 2025 report *Total Experience:
Redefining Excellence in the Age of Agentic AI*, published **12 June 2026**. This is a further
instalment of the same serially-released commercial sweep that produced the EY Canada
investigation (src-0032), and it documents a **fourth named institution** for half (A) of
`institutional-incident-real-world-impact` — after ENISA (public agency), EY Canada (Big Four
commercial) and curl (open-source project).

Its analytic significance is larger than "one more incident", for two reasons:

1. **IT PUBLISHES A COMPLETE PER-REPORT CITATION DENOMINATOR AND CATEGORISED BREAKDOWN** —
   45 citations, partitioned 5 / 28 / 12 — which src-0032 could not supply for EY because of
   that page's JavaScript odometer defect. This is the first GPTZero instalment this base holds
   whose numerals are **machine-readable in text mode**.
2. **THE NUMERALS SURVIVED A TWO-RENDER CHECK.** Fetched at cycle 99 on both the direct render
   and `r.jina.ai`. Both returned `45`, `only five`, `28`, `12` and `40 of 45` **verbatim and
   identical**. This page does **not** exhibit src-0032's odometer defect for the body numerals.

**IT RECORDS NO INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE.** Asked directly for any sentence describing what KPMG
did (withdrew, pulled, corrected, declined to comment) and for any passage presented as a direct
quotation from KPMG, the page returned **ABSENT for both, on the direct render**. The KPMG
response is documented elsewhere — see src-0035 and src-0036.

# Limitations

- **VENDOR PAGE: SIMULTANEOUSLY THE EVIDENCE AND THE MARKETING FOR ITS OWN INSTRUMENT.** GPTZero
  sells AI detection. Every standing caveat recorded against src-0032 applies here unchanged. The
  counts are GPTZero's own and have not been independently reproduced by this base.
- **"40 of 45 are fake" IS A LOSSY SUMMARY OF THE PAGE'S OWN PARTITION.** 12 of those 40 are
  explicitly "too vague or flawed to **accurately determine if a source exists**" — i.e.
  *undetermined*, not *determined-fake*. **The strictly-supported floor is 28 of 45 defective
  with 12 undetermined; "40 of 45 fake" is the page's rounding-up of its own data.** A cycle
  citing 40/45 must carry this sentence with it. This is the same shape of defect the base has
  filed repeatedly.
- **PERCENTAGE FIGURES ARE RENDER-DEPENDENT AND MUST NOT BE CITED.** The direct render returned
  `"89% Flawed"` and `"56% Mixed"`; the `r.jina.ai` render returned **`'89%'` ABSENT**. The
  renders **disagree**, and these figures sit in the same metric-tile furniture that produced
  src-0032's odometer defect. **No cycle may cite 89% or 56% from this source.** The body-text
  counts (45 / 5 / 28 / 12 / 40 of 45) are a different matter and *did* reproduce on both
  renders.
- **THE `r.jina.ai` RENDER REPORTED A DIGIT-RUN PRESENT** (the numbered 1–45 citation list) where
  the direct render reported none. Cycle 99 reads this as the enumerated citation list rather
  than an odometer artefact, but flags it: **this is an inference, not a reading.**
- **NO METHODOLOGY STATEMENT WAS EXTRACTED** at cycle 99. Whether the "manually verified" human
  check recorded for the EY instalment (src-0032) was also applied here is **not established**
  and must not be assumed by transfer.
- **NO RESPONSE, NO AFTERMATH, NO DISCLOSURE-POLICY MATERIAL.** See key claim 3.
- **PER ctr-0051, NO CYCLE MAY CITE A COUNT FOR src-0028**; that standing prohibition is about a
  different source and does not extend to this one, but the general rule it encodes — that an
  *enumerated count* returned by this tooling is not evidence of a count — is why claim 1 rests on
  **verbatim sentences containing the numerals**, which both renders returned identically, rather
  than on any count this agent performed.
