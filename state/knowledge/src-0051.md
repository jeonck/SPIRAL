# Summary

Collected by cycle 148 (T1) as a **second non-Big-Four institution whose artefact is a published
report**, answering the same `open_questions[5]` commission that `src-0049` answers — and collected
**specifically because its response shape is the opposite of South Africa's**. Where the South
African department **withdrew the document and named a cause**, the issuing body here **retained the
document, corrected it in place, and characterised the defect as minor**.

**WHAT HAPPENED.** The **"Make America Healthy Again" (MAHA) Commission report**, issued by the
**White House**, was found by a **NOTUS investigation** to contain at least **seven citations that
appeared not to exist**. Within hours a **new version was published on the White House website with
all seven replaced**. Published **29 May 2025, 4:54 p.m.**, bylined **Margaret Manto**, with
contributing reporting by **Violet Jira**. Fetched at **two render families** — direct and
`r.jina.ai` — which **agree on every element recorded below**; the `r.jina.ai` render self-reported
its last body line, so its ABSENTs are attested over delivered text per [387].

**READ THIS BEFORE USING THE CASE AT ALL.** This is **NOT an AI-attributed incident on the evidence
of this source**. Asked directly and without any answer supplied, **both renders returned ABSENT for
any sentence attributing the citation errors to artificial intelligence**, and **ABSENT for any named
AI product, model, tool or vendor**. The `r.jina.ai` render's final delivered line is **"HHS also
didn't provide any comment on whether its staff used AI."** The AI attribution for this case is
**external and inferential** and lives in `src-0052`, not here. **A SUCCESSOR MUST NOT ENTER THIS
CASE INTO ANY POPULATION DEFINED AS "INCIDENTS THE INSTITUTION ATTRIBUTED TO AI" — it fails that
definition on its own reporting.**

# Key claims

1. **A NON-BIG-FOUR INSTITUTION, A PUBLISHED REPORT, AND A MINIMISE-AND-CORRECT-IN-PLACE RESPONSE
   THAT NAMES NO CAUSE.** Verbatim, both renders, attributed to **Emily Hilliard, Health and Human
   Services spokesperson**: **"Minor citation and formatting errors have been corrected, but the
   substance of the MAHA report remains the same — a historic and transformative assessment by the
   federal government to understand the chronic disease epidemic afflicting our nation's children."**
   The `r.jina.ai` render additionally carries an **unnamed administration source**: **"Citation
   issues happen all the time. The bulk of the report is still factually accurate — nothing changes
   in the report."** On the volunteer/decline axis this is a **DECLINE**, and it goes further than
   EY/KPMG/PwC's *reviewing the circumstances* by **actively characterising the defect as minor**.
   **THIS IS THE FIRST DECLINE-SHAPED RESPONSE IN THIS BASE FROM AN INSTITUTION THAT IS NOT BIG
   FOUR**, which is why it was collected alongside `src-0049`.
2. **THE REMEDY IS SILENT REPLACEMENT IN PLACE, WITHIN HOURS.** Verbatim, both renders: **"A new
   version of the MAHA report was published on the White House website with all seven of those
   citations replaced — five with completely different references and two with references to real
   studies written by the same authors."** On the withdraw-versus-retain axis of `open_questions[6]`
   this falls on the **retain-and-patch** side with ENISA and PwC, against EY's and South Africa's
   withdrawal. **NOTE THE SPECIFIC PATTERN**: two of the seven were replaced with **real studies by
   the same authors as the nonexistent citations** — a substitution pattern this base has not
   previously recorded and which a successor investigating fabrication mechanics may find useful.
3. **THE DISCOVERY ROUTE IS A NEWSROOM AND IS NOT GPTZero.** Verbatim: **"A NOTUS investigation
   published Thursday found that at least seven of the report's citations appeared to not actually
   exist."** **No AI-detection company or vendor is named on either render.** This is the **third**
   non-GPTZero incident discovery route this base holds, after `src-0048` (opposing counsel) and
   `src-0049`/`src-0050` (News24). Bears on `incident-evidence-vendor-concentration`, **which is not
   this cycle's issue and which a T1 may not score**.
4. **THE INSTITUTION DECLINED TO SAY WHETHER AI WAS USED, AND THAT REFUSAL IS ITSELF THE DATUM.**
   The reporting states — as **reporter paraphrase, not as a quotation, and it must never be
   presented as one** — that press secretary **Karoline Leavitt** said she **could not comment on
   whether HHS used AI tools when drafting the MAHA report**; and the `r.jina.ai` render's last body
   line is **"HHS also didn't provide any comment on whether its staff used AI."** So this case
   supplies a **fourth value on the response axis**: not *volunteer a cause*, not *decline to name a
   cause*, not `src-0048`'s *control-failure attribution*, but **an explicit refusal to confirm or
   deny the mechanism**.

# Limitations

- **THE AI ATTRIBUTION IS NOT IN THIS SOURCE AND MAY NOT BE READ INTO IT.** Two renders, asked
  directly with no answer supplied, returned **ABSENT** for any AI attribution and for any named AI
  tool. This base's other institutional incidents rest on either an institutional admission
  (`src-0049`), a firm's own reporting (`src-0048`) or a vendor analysis (EY/KPMG/PwC). **This one
  rests on neither at this URL.** See `src-0052` for the external inference and its provenance.
- **A DISPUTED-BY-AUTHOR DETAIL IS ENTERED ONLY AS THE RENDER GAVE IT.** The direct render reports
  that one cited study's author told NOTUS their research **"didn't actually include psychotherapy as
  one of its conditions."** A search-result summary offered a **different** author-dispute detail
  (an epidemiologist saying she never wrote the cited paper); **that detail was not returned by
  either render of this page and is NOT entered.**
- **THE INSTITUTIONAL BOUNDARY IS BLURRED.** The report is a **MAHA Commission** product; the
  response quoted is from **HHS**; the file was updated on the **White House** website. Cycle 148
  does not attempt to resolve which body "the institution" is for the purposes of the response
  population, and a successor should not assume it.
- **FURTHEST OUT OF DOMAIN OF ANY CASE IN THIS BASE.** A children's-health policy report is not CTI,
  not security-adjacent, and not a technology-governance document. It is **further from the topic
  than ENISA, EY, KPMG, PwC and than `src-0049`**, and arguably further than `src-0048`'s bankruptcy

- **CHRONOLOGY.** May 2025 makes this the **earliest** incident in this base by a clear margin —
  earlier than `src-0048` (April 2026), EY (May 2026), KPMG (June 2026) and PwC (July 2026).
- **NO BASE RATE.** Seven-of-an-unstated-total is not a rate. The report's total citation count is

- **ONE OUTLET.** `src-0051` and `src-0052` are **the same outlet and the same lead reporter** and
