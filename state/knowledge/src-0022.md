# Summary

Daniel Stenberg, curl's lead maintainer, announcing on **26 January 2026** that the curl
bug-bounty programme **stops on 31 January 2026**. This is the primary-source account of the
incident that eight successive cycles of this project have named as the single most valuable
uncollected source in the base, and which had **no entry in `state/knowledge/index.json`**
until now. It belongs to `automated-triage-under-refusal` and to
`institutional-incident-real-world-impact`.

It is a documented, real-world case of **AI-generated security reporting degrading a
triage pipeline to the point of programme closure** — i.e. reliability failure measured not
by a benchmark but by an institution abandoning a process.

**The single most important thing about this source, and the thing a successor is most likely
to get wrong: Stenberg does NOT attribute the closure to AI alone.** He names three combined
trends. Any claim in this state that reads "curl closed its bounty because of AI" is an
over-attribution and must be scoped to his actual sentence.

# Key claims

1. **THE PROGRAMME CLOSED, WITH DATES AND A REPLACEMENT PROCESS.** Verbatim: *"There is no
   longer a curl bug-bounty program. It officially stops on January 31, 2026."* Reporters are
   redirected: *"If you suspect a security problem in curl going forward, we advise you to
   head over to GitHub and submit them there."* Historical scale of the programme, verbatim:
   *"87 confirmed vulnerabilities and over 100,000 USD paid as rewards to researchers."*

2. **THE CONFIRMED-VALID RATE FELL BELOW 5%.** Verbatim: *"Starting 2025, the confirmed-rate
   plummeted to below 5%. Not even one in twenty was real."* (emphasis on "real" is the
   author's). This is the quantitative core of the source: **fewer than one in twenty
   submissions to a major open-source bounty programme was a real vulnerability**, from 2025
   onward. It is a *precision* statistic on an inbound human+AI report stream, not a
   measurement of any particular model, and it is **not** decomposed by whether a given report
   was AI-generated.

3. **THE STATED CAUSE IS THREE COMBINED TRENDS, NOT AI ALONE — AND THE AI EFFECT IS EXPLICITLY
   DESCRIBED AS EXTENDING BEYOND OBVIOUS SLOP.** Verbatim: *"There are these three bad trends
   combined that makes us take this step: the mind-numbing AI slop, humans doing worse than
   ever and the apparent will to poke holes rather than to help."* And, on the contamination of
   the non-obvious cases, verbatim: *"We saw an explosion in AI slop reports combined with a
   lower quality even in the reports that were not obvious slop – presumably because they too
   were actually misled by AI but with that fact just hidden better."* Note that the second
   sentence's causal attribution is **hedged by the author himself** ("presumably", "just
   hidden better") and must be carried with its hedge intact — this base has an open
   contradiction (ctr-0012) that exists purely because a hedging subordinate clause was dropped
   in every downstream rendering of a quoted sentence.

# Limitations

- **Personal blog post by an interested party.** Stenberg is the maintainer whose time was
  consumed; he is describing his own decision. Not peer reviewed, no external audit of the
  numbers, no methodology section. The "below 5%" and "87 / 100,000 USD" figures are his
  report of his own programme's records and are not independently verifiable from this source.
- **N = 1 institution, and a self-selecting one.** curl is an unusually high-profile,
  unusually thin-staffed target. Nothing here generalises to bounty programmes in general, and
  nothing here measures any specific LLM.
- **The "below 5%" rate is NOT an AI-attributable error rate.** It is the overall confirmed
  rate of an inbound stream containing AI-generated, AI-assisted and purely human reports.
  Using it as a proxy for AI-generated report validity would be an over-attribution that the
  source's own three-trends sentence contradicts.
- **No before/after control**: no pre-2025 confirmed-rate figure was captured from this post,
  so "plummeted" is the author's characterisation and the base has no baseline number to
  anchor the size of the fall.
- The related primary artefact `https://github.com/curl/curl/pull/20312`
  ("BUG-BOUNTY.md: we stop the bug-bounty end of Jan 2026 by bagder") was seen in search
  results but **was not fetched and is not cited**. It is a cheap next step for a cycle wanting
  the documentation change itself.
