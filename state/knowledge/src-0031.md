## Summary

400 autonomous penetration-testing runs — four models, 100 runs each — against one
unchanged honeypot hosting OWASP Juice Shop, weak SSH credentials and anonymous FTP,
with prompt, orchestrator and target fixed. The paper measures how much the *outcome*
varies across identical repetitions and finds that it varies a great deal, with
model-distinctive failure modes.

## Key claims (verbatim-supported)

1. **Outcome under identical repeated conditions is highly variable and the variation is
   model-distinctive.** Abstract, verbatim: "Claude achieved full exploitation in 61 of
   100 runs; Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite in 85; GPT-4o-mini in 56 while deploying 98 unique
   attack strategies; qwen2.5-coder:14b in 25. Failure modes are model-distinctive:
   Claude through API truncation (39 runs), qwen through premature completion (52),
   GPT-4o-mini through iteration-budget exhaustion (23)." Per-service rates with
   confidence intervals, Table 2, verbatim: "Claude Sonnet: SQLi 69.0% [59.4, 77.2], SSH
   63.0% [53.2, 71.8], FTP 61.0% [51.2, 70.0]"; "Gemini Flash-Lite: SQLi 92.0% [85.0,
   95.9], SSH 92.0% [85.0, 95.9]"; "GPT-4o-mini: FTP 98.0% [93.0, 99.4]";
   "qwen2.5-coder:14b: Creds read 60.0% [50.2, 69.1]".

2. **NO model-expressed confidence, and NO proper-scoring calibration metric of any
   kind.** A fetch asked directly whether the paper reports model-expressed confidence,
   ECE, Brier score, reliability diagrams or an abstention measure, and returned
   **ABSENT** for ECE, Brier and reliability diagrams. Its uncertainty constructs are
   **behavioural**: cross-run outcome variability, and refusal counts. This places
   src-0031 on the **same side of the open_questions[8] modality axis as src-0018 and
   src-0002** — off it entirely. It therefore **cannot corroborate src-0001's
   token-probability calibration**, and it does not disturb the finding that src-0001
   remains the only source in this base measuring proper-scoring calibration on a CTI
   task.

3. **A refusal/abstention finding, and a documented self-correction about it that is
   itself the more transferable datum.** Section 4.4, verbatim: "Across 400 runs and four
   providers, no model produced a content refusal under the 'authorized penetration
   testing' framing used in this study." Abstract, verbatim, on how an earlier version
   got this wrong: "Claude Sonnet 4's API calls did encounter upstream service
   unavailability - 91 of 1,135 calls returned HTTP 529 overloaded_error during a
   documented Anthropic capacity event, truncating 39 of 100 Claude runs. An earlier
   draft catalogued these as safety refusals; on full-log audit they are upstream API
   failures, not model-level refusals."

## Limitations — read these before citing, several are severe

- **UNREVIEWED SINGLE-AUTHOR PREPRINT.** No journal reference on the arXiv metadata.
- **TEMPERATURE IS NOT ZERO.** 0.3 for three models and **1.0** for Claude. So its
  run-to-run variation includes **deliberate sampling** as a first-order contributor —
  precisely the mechanism src-0030 says T=0 removes. This is NOT a measurement of
  irreducible instability, and it must not be compared like-for-like against any
  base-rate figure from a T=0 design. The asymmetry between Claude at 1.0 and the others
  at 0.3 also means **cross-model comparisons in this paper are confounded by sampling
  temperature**, which the paper does not appear to control for.
- **CLAUDE'S NUMBERS ARE CONTAMINATED BY AN INFRASTRUCTURE EVENT THE PAPER ITSELF
  DOCUMENTS.** 39 of 100 Claude runs were truncated by upstream HTTP 529 errors during a
  capacity event, and the paper further records that "Claude's exploitation success rate
  declined significantly across the 7-hour" window for that reason. Claude's 61/100 is
  therefore a floor contaminated by API availability, not a clean behavioural
  measurement.
- Author-stated limitations, verbatim, Section 5.5: "All experiments use a single
  honeypot configuration"; "Prompt framing is known to substantially affect model
  behavior; this study isolates run-to-run variability by fixing the prompt";
  "Cloud-hosted LLM behavior is subject to provider-side updates...Exact
  replication...may not be possible"; "History trimming asymmetry" between cloud and
  local models; "The 25-iteration limit artificially truncates GPT-4o-mini runs".
- **The refusal finding is scoped to one framing.** "no model produced a content refusal
  under the 'authorized penetration testing' framing used in this study" is a statement
  about that framing, on one target, with an orchestrator that issued a "one-shot
  authorization re-prompt". It is not a general under-refusal rate and must not be cited
  as one.
- Offensive-security setting. This base's topic explicitly excludes offensive techniques;
  this source is used **only** for its repeat-run consistency methodology and its
  refusal/abstention bookkeeping, never for attack content.
