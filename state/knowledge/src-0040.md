# Limitations

- **DOWNSTREAM OF GPTZERO, LIKE EVERY OTHER COMMERCIAL CASE IN THIS BASE.** Key claim 4 records
  the attribution in the outlet's own words. EY, KPMG and PwC **all** trace to the same vendor;
  City A.M. adds an **outlet**, not an **investigation**. `incident-evidence-vendor-concentration`
  governs, and no cycle may treat this source as independent corroboration of GPTZero's findings.
  **What it independently carries is the PwC STATEMENT**, which GPTZero's page does not have at
  all — that is this source's whole value and the limit of it.
- **A PRESS STATEMENT, NOT A PRIMARY ARTEFACT.** As with EY and KPMG, the base holds **no
  PwC-published page** carrying this text. The wording is what the outlet reports PwC said, and it
  is **single-outlet**: cycle 104 did not fetch a second outlet carrying the same statement, so
  unlike KPMG's third sentence, **no sentence here is cross-outlet confirmed**. It is two-**render**
  confirmed, which is a different and weaker thing, and the distinction is deliberate.
- **"is updating" DESCRIBES AN UNFINISHED PROCESS.** As of 29 July 2026 — seven days before this
  fetch — the correction was **announced, not completed**. **This base has NOT checked whether any
  citation in any of the four PwC reports was in fact updated**, and makes no claim that it was.
  Checking the four reports themselves is a named, cheap, untried route.
- **"a limited number" IS PwC'S OWN CHARACTERISATION AND IS NOT A COUNT.** It must never be
  rendered as a numeral, and it cannot be compared with EY's, KPMG's or ENISA's citation figures.
  **No count is taken from this source at all.**
- **NO REMOVAL CLAIM, IN EITHER DIRECTION.** The page does not say the reports were removed, and
  it does not say they remain online. Cycle 104 asked and got no sentence either way, so
  **correct-in-place is read from what PwC says it is DOING, not from a verified absence of
  removal.** A cycle wanting the stronger claim must check PwC's own sites.
- **PwC MIDDLE EAST IS A CONSULTANCY, NOT A CTI PUBLISHER PROPER.** The standing scope caveat
  carried for EY since cycle 12 and for KPMG since cycle 99 applies here identically.
- **THE 100 PER CENT IN THE HEADLINE IS A DETECTOR PROBABILITY**, not a finding about citation
  accuracy — see `open_questions[7]`, the numeral-collision parking entry, and src-0037 key
  claim 3. It is quoted here as part of the headline and is **not** entered as a claim.
- **DOES NOT BEAR ON open_questions[0].** A response is not a base rate. This source closes
  nothing about prevalence across CTI publishers.
