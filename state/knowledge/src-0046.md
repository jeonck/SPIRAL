## What the study does

- **Setting:** sequential (lifelong) supervised fine-tuning of an LLM across a task stream, with the
  model re-evaluated on every previously seen task's test split after each step.
- **Models:** Pythia (410M, 1.4B), Llama-3 (1B, 8B), Mistral 7B.
- **Datasets:** GLUE, SuperGLUE, CaseHOLD, MedQA, MMLU, news classification, HumanEval, MBPP.
- **Metrics, verbatim from Section 4.1:** *"we evaluate the current model on every previously seen
  task's test split and record accuracy, expected calibration error, and empirical conformal
  coverage at nominal level 1−α=0.9"*.
- **NO MODEL OVERLAP with src-0001** (gpt4o, gemini-1.5-pro-latest, mistral-large-2). Mistral 7B is
  not mistral-large-2; do not treat this as a per-model replication.

## Key claims (verbatim-supported)

**KC1 — the thesis, from the abstract. CONFIRMED ON BOTH RENDERS.** Verbatim:

> "Continual learning for large language models is typically evaluated through accuracy retention
> under sequential fine-tuning. We argue that this perspective is incomplete, because uncertainty
> reliability can degrade earlier and more sharply than top-1 performance."

**KC2 — the quantified collapse, and it happens after ONE fine-tuning step. CONFIRMED ON BOTH
RENDERS, IDENTICAL WORDING.** From Section 4.2, verbatim:

> "Mean coverage at nominal level 0.9 drops from 0.90 to 0.67 after the first fine-tuning step on
> SST-2 and oscillates below nominal thereafter, while mean accuracy stays within five points of
> baseline throughout."

**READ THE SECOND CLAUSE AS CAREFULLY AS THE FIRST.** Accuracy holds to within five points while
coverage falls 23 points. This is the same dissociation src-0001 reports between accuracy and
calibration under fine-tuning, on a different uncertainty metric.

**KC3 — the ratio, which is the paper's headline number. CONFIRMED ON BOTH RENDERS.** Abstract:
*"coverage loss exceeds accuracy loss by a factor of roughly 3.4×±0.5×"*; the proxy render gives the
body form verbatim: *"Across the seven classification-style scenarios, the ratio of coverage loss to
accuracy loss averages 3.4 ± 0.5×"*. **THE SCOPE IS SEVEN CLASSIFICATION-STYLE SCENARIOS** — quote
the body form, which carries that scope, in preference to the abstract form, which does not.

**KC4 — elicitation modality, recorded verbatim at source as ctr-0027 requires.** Uncertainty is
**token-probability-derived, not verbalized**. Verbatim (proxy render):

> "For each task Tk with label space Yk, the nonconformity score sθ (x, y ) = − log fθ (y | x) is the
> negative log-probability the model assigns to label y given x"

Same modality family as src-0001 and src-0045; **contrast src-0013, which is verbalized**, and
src-0018, which elicits no model-expressed confidence at all.

## Limitations

**Stated by the authors, Section 8 (Discussion), verbatim and identical on both renders:**

> "results are for sequential supervised fine-tuning, not continual pre-training, federated
> adaptation, or unlearning; formal results are for classification-style tasks with task-specific
> buffers, with open-ended generation exploratory in Appendix B"

The proxy render adds: *"the recovery theorem assumes access to labeled calibration data per task,
which is standard for continual-learning benchmarks but may not hold in all deployments"*.

**Agent findings, not the authors':**

1. **THE METRIC IS THE BINDING LIMIT.** Conformal coverage is not a proper score. Coverage answers
   "does the prediction set contain the truth at the nominal rate", not "is the stated probability
   right". `open_questions[11]` stays open on the proper-score leg after this source.
2. **NO NUMERIC ECE.** Figure 4 is captioned *"ECE trajectories for Pythia-1.4B GLUE. Tasks with more
   label classes (MNLI) show larger ECE spikes."* — a figure, with no values. **DO NOT READ NUMBERS
   OFF IT.**
3. **Open-ended generation is "exploratory" by the authors' own admission**, and the formal results
   are classification-only. src-0001's worst calibration failures are on generation. Same transfer
   gap as src-0045, from the other direction.
4. **Not peer-reviewed**, and three months old at time of collection. No independent replication
   was searched for this cycle.
5. The intervention is *sequential* fine-tuning across a task stream. src-0001 fine-tunes once. The
   mechanism producing coverage collapse here may be forgetting-driven and specific to the
   continual setting; that is untested against a single-step baseline in what this cycle read.
